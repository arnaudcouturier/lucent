# Lucent 0.3.0-beta.2

The Profile step becomes **Graphics**: instead of four profiles, you choose the settings themselves.

- **DLSS preset:** DLAA, Ultra Quality, Quality, Balanced, Performance or Ultra Performance, with the resolution XIV renders at on your screen shown under the choice.
- **Neural Rendering resolution:** 75%, 100% (recommended) or 125%. With DLAA, 75% is suggested to protect your frame rate; it never switches by itself.
- **Passes:** 1, 2 or 3.
- **Spatial compression** and **residual frame generation** are switches with a plain explanation of each. Residual frame generation is Experimental and is offered on RTX 40 and 50 cards with frame generation at 2× or higher.
- **Unlock multi-frame generation** (RTX 40, Experimental) is its own switch. Off gives Off and 2×; on adds 3×–6× and Dynamic, and 2× works with either.
- The FPS overlay toggles with **Page Up** (content 2026.10.16 writes `[Menu] FpsShortcutKey = 0x21`); the Keys screen and the Done screen show it. Page Down stays XIV’s zoom key.
- Review and Done show the picks on one line, for example "Quality · 2 passes · 100%".
- Settings: the Reset setup and Uninstall cards are now separated by a gap.

Beta content **2026.10.16** requires this app (0.3.0-beta.1 was superseded the same day: its Done screen did not teach the FPS key). It ships the same files as 2026.10.15, so nothing new is downloaded; existing `OptiScaler.ini` files keep their values on update, and **Settings → Reset setup…** offers the new choices. Content 2026.10.15 and its profiles keep working for rollback.
